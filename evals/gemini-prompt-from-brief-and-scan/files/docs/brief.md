# Brief: PantryPal

**Product.** A mobile app that helps households track what is in their pantry and fridge, warns before food expires, and suggests meals from what is already at home.

**Audience.** Households of 2 to 5 people in Western Europe who cook at home at least four times a week, mostly aged 28 to 45.

**Key difference.** Shared household inventory that updates from receipt photos, so nobody types items by hand.

**Goal.** Decide whether to build an MVP and which segment to target first.

**Constraints.** Two-person team, 4 months to MVP, freemium model considered.
